# Application Enhancements & Feature Requests

## Home Dashboard
*   **Rename Stream Section:** Rename *Active Technology Streams* to **Streams**.
*   **Stream Cards Navigation:** Clicking on a Stream card must open a dedicated page for that stream. 
    *   This page should display **stream analytics** (article/news velocity, new/unique ideas, or signals) and **summaries**.
    *   Summaries must allow navigation down to the unique articles ingested within that stream.
*   **Cleanup:** Remove *Crawler Telemetry*.
*   **Editorial Highlights:** Restructure this section to present the **top 3 hottest themes** across each individual stream.

## NewsLetter Library
*   **Tagging Corrections:** Stop tagging all article cards as "General AI". Fix this so each article card is accurately tagged by its specific **stream** alongside any relevant **functional tags**.
*   **Metrics & Icons:** Remove the confidence score from the article cards. Instead, display a **fire icon** 🔥 if the article is being covered by multiple sources.
*   **AI Analyst Commentary Fixes:**
    *   **UI/UX:** Eliminate the excessive scrolling currently required to view the commentary. Clicking the commentary should open a clean, modern **popup** instead.
    *   **Formatting:** Ensure the commentary renders using premium formatting.
*   **Data Cleaning (Titles):** 
    *   Fix HTML entities and special characters in titles (e.g., convert `&amp;` to `&`).
    *   Strip out any embedded dates from the article titles.
*   **Card Timestamp Logic:** Each article card is a summary of similar/same articles ingested across sources. Update the card date to reflect the **last coverage date** of any underlying article within that card.
*   **Theme & Drill-Down Architecture:** Article cards represent themes. 
    *   Clicking a theme must open a **Theme Card** showing its underlying, semantically similar article groupings.
    *   Clicking an article grouping should allow users to see the underlying articles and drill down directly to the original ingested article.

## Ingestion Operations
### 1. Ingestion Monitoring & Alerts
*   **Dynamic Execution History:** Implement a real-time, dynamic view showing which sources are currently being ingested and which ones are queued.
*   **Alerting System:** Add ingestion alerts to notify administrators if specific sources fail to ingest properly or throw errors.

### 2. Analytics Sub-Page
*   Create a dedicated sub-page within Ingestion Operations to track:
    *   Time elapsed per source / per ingestion run.
    *   Total tokens utilized.
    *   Volume ingested (e.g., number of articles segmented by source and filtered by day, week, month, quarter, and year).

### 3. Ingestion Mechanism Rearchitecture
*   **3.1 Storage:** Crawlers must visit each source, download the entire article, and store it directly in the PostgreSQL database.
*   **3.2 Asset Extraction:** Extract, tag, and store any graphs, charts, or visuals alongside a reference link to the parent article/source. Ensure these assets are available for use in *Assemble Edition*, *Oracle Ball*, and chatbot responses.
*   **3.3 Vector Search:** Ensure all downloaded articles are searchable and queryable. Generate embeddings for every article and store them in `pgvector`.
*   **3.4 Agentic Processing:** Process ingested articles via agents to group them into themes (where a theme consists of groups of semantically similar articles). A single stream can contain multiple themes.

## Oracle Ball Predictions
*   **Automated Refreshes:** Predictions must automatically generate and refresh immediately following an ingestion run. Remove the confusing requirement forcing users to manually ingest and publish articles in the newsletter library to sync predictions to the oracle roadmap.
